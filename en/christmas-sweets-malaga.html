What are borrachuelos?

Borrachuelos are Malaga's signature Christmas sweet: a dough made with olive oil and sweet Moscatel wine from Malaga, filled with «cabello de ángel» (angel-hair squash jam), fried and rolled in sugar or dipped in honey. The name comes from «borracho» (drunk), because of the wine in the dough.

What are pestiños?

Pestiños are folded strips of dough flavoured with sesame and aniseed, fried in olive oil and glazed with honey. They are one of the oldest sweets in Andalusia and are eaten at Christmas and Easter.

Buñuelos and almond cake

Buñuelos de viento are light fried dough puffs, hollow inside and coated in sugar and cinnamon. Almond cake (torta de almendra) is a simple bake of ground almonds, egg, sugar, olive oil and cinnamon.
